# QR Scanner

QR Scanner is a simple web app for scanning QR codes and creating your
own. I built it to make everyday QR tasks quick and easy, without
needing to create an account.

## What it can do

-   Scan QR codes using your device's camera.
-   Scan a QR code from an uploaded image.
-   Create QR codes for text, websites, email addresses, phone numbers,
    Wi-Fi details, and contact information.
-   Download generated QR codes as PNG images.
-   View, search, delete, and export your scan history.
-   Switch between light and dark mode.
-   Install the app on supported devices.

## How to use it

Open the website in your browser.

To scan a code, allow camera access and choose **Start Camera**, or
upload an image containing a QR code.

To create a code, open the **Generate** section, choose what kind of QR
code you want, enter the details, and select **Generate QR Code**. You
can then download the image.

## Run it locally

Download or clone this repository and serve the project folder using a
local web server. Open the local address in your browser.

Camera access and service workers generally require a secure context,
such as `localhost` or an HTTPS website.

## Privacy

Scan history and app settings are saved in your browser on your device.
This project does not require an account or its own backend server.
